# Point of view

Copy this folder to ~/Documents/heard/kb (or the folder set in Settings) and rewrite it for your company. This file
goes into every Ask prompt in full, so keep it under two pages and write opinions, not a brochure. The cards carry
the detail; this is the voice.

## Who we are, in one breath

Two or three sentences: what you sell, who it is for, what you never do. Say what is different plainly.

## What we believe

- One belief per line, as the sentence you would say on a call.
- Name the thing you recommend and the thing you argue against.
- Keep the reasons short; the cards explain.

## How we sell

- What the engagement looks like and what it is not.
- What you say when the product doesn't fit.
- What is never written down on an early call (pricing, timelines you don't own).

## How we answer the usual objections

- "We built this ourselves." Your two-sentence answer.
- "We use <competitor>." Where they are strong, where they aren't, when to move on.
- "Where is the UI?" Say it before they do.

## Phrases we use

The handful of phrases that sound like you.

## Words we don't use

Words that make the answer sound like marketing. A claim must be true today, specific, and clear to a sixth grader.
